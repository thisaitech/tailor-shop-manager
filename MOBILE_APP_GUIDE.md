# How to Install as a Mobile App

This Tailor Shop Management application is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)**, which means you can install it on your mobile device and use it just like a native app!

## 📱 Installation Instructions

### For Android Devices (Chrome Browser)

1. **Open the app** in Chrome browser on your Android device
2. Look for the **"Add to Home Screen"** banner that appears at the bottom
3. Tap **"Install"** or **"Add"**
4. Confirm by tapping **"Add"** again
5. The app icon will appear on your home screen

**Alternative method:**
1. Open the app in Chrome
2. Tap the **three dots menu** (⋮) in the top-right corner
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install app"**
4. Name the app and tap **"Add"**

### For iPhone/iPad (Safari Browser)

1. **Open the app** in Safari browser
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
4. Name the app (e.g., "Tailor Shop")
5. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner
6. The app icon will appear on your home screen

### For Desktop (Chrome, Edge, or Brave)

1. **Open the app** in your browser
2. Look for the **install icon** (⊕) in the address bar
3. Click it and select **"Install"**
4. The app will open in its own window

## ✨ Benefits of Installing

- **Works Offline**: Access your data even without internet
- **Home Screen Icon**: Launch directly from your home screen
- **Full Screen**: Uses the entire screen without browser UI
- **Fast Access**: Opens instantly like a native app
- **Push Notifications**: Get updates about orders (future feature)
- **Data Persistence**: All your data is saved locally on your device

## 🔒 Privacy & Data

- All data is stored **locally on your device**
- No data is sent to external servers
- Uninstalling the app removes all data
- You can export your data before uninstalling (future feature)

## 📊 Device Requirements

- **Android**: Chrome 72+ or Edge 79+
- **iOS**: Safari 15.4+ (iOS 15.4 or later)
- **Desktop**: Chrome 73+, Edge 79+, or Brave

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**"Add to Home Screen" option not showing?**
- Make sure you're using a supported browser
- Check that you're accessing the app via HTTPS
- Try refreshing the page

**App not working offline?**
- Make sure you've opened the app at least once while online
- Check your browser's site settings allow offline storage

**Want to update the app?**
- Simply open it while connected to the internet
- The app will automatically update to the latest version

## 🗑️ How to Uninstall

### Android
- Long press the app icon
- Select "App info" or "Remove"
- Tap "Uninstall"

### iOS
- Long press the app icon
- Tap "Remove App"
- Select "Delete App"

### Desktop
- Right-click the app icon in your taskbar/dock
- Select "Uninstall"
